7. Anchoring Detail

Each chair stands on four exposed M10 security bolts; nothing else ties it to the ground.

7.1Visible

Only a 17.5 mm domed head shows above the foot.

It is painted to match the chair. Front bolts sit under the seat; rear bolts sit in the 82 mm gap between rear leg and back, out of normal sight.

7.2Removable

Undo four bolts and the chair lifts away.

The resin-anchored sleeve stays flush with the paving and takes a plastic plug. On the short rear foot (33 mm flat) the hole is cut before bending and checked with a test fold.
