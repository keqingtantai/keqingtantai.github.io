10. In Motion

A six-second sideways walk past Chair A.

10.1What it shows

A small step is enough to set the rings moving.

Every 31.5 mm of sideways movement inverts the image, so a slow walk or a sway of the head turns the static grating into a moving one.

10.2Limits

The effect belongs to a place, not to every viewpoint.

It reads best about 2.75–3.3 m behind Chair A and fades outside that band. A person sitting on Chair B hides it, and low sun may cast slot shadows that compete with the image.
