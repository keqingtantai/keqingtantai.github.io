Walking Again
- A rollator rebuilt from one scrapped shared bike
Designer: Keqing Tantai
KEYWORDS
ABSTRACT
  • Reuse before recycling
  • Shared-bike afterlife
  • Rollator
  • Ageing in place
  • Distributed manufacturing
  • Design for disassembly
  • Embodied carbon
  • Parametric adaptation

Walking Again turns one scrapped shared bike into a four-wheel rollator for older people. Instead of shredding the bike and remelting its alloy, the design keeps the parts whose value lies in their form: the welded aluminium rear triangle, seat post, handlebar, saddle and baskets. Turned back to front, the rear triangle becomes the spine; one saw cut, straight steel tubes and catalogue clamps complete the frame. Every joint is bolted, so the walker can be built and repaired in a neighbourhood workshop with hand tools. The detailed model was checked for strength and stability, and direct reuse saves about 15 kg CO₂e per walker compared with remelting the same parts.
