8. Wall Edition

The mechanism lies flat beside the axis instead of stacking behind it, so the clock sits 35.5 mm off the wall.

8.1No slip ring

The head turns 330° a day and rewinds at midnight.

Because the beam never has to pass 24:00 going forward, the two LED wires run straight down the 3 mm tube and twist by less than one turn. Dropping the slip ring and the coupler removed most of the original 80 mm stack.

8.2Drive beside the axis

A 60-tooth gear sinks into a pocket in the dial.

A 28BYJ-48 stepper bolts face-down to the back of the dial and drives the tube through a 12/60 spur pair. Two MR63ZZ bearings carry the tube; a magnet in the gear and a hall sensor find 00:00. A TMC2209 driver keeps the moves near silent.

8.3Form

A saucer head, a sloped rim and a back that flares away.

The back shell curves inward from the dial edge to a Ø 108 mm face, just large enough for the motor, so from the side the clock reads as a thin disc. The hardwood is specified to ±0.05 mm, with crests and wall backs left unsanded.
