Pets Clock

- A 24-hour wall clock told by cast shadows

Designer: Keqing Tantai

KEYWORDS

ABSTRACT

  • Cast shadow
  • Point light source
  • Inverse relief design
  • Pixel tessellation
  • 24-hour dial
  • Light as an hour hand
  • CNC wood relief
  • Computational design

A clock usually shows time with hands; this one shows it with shadows. A single LED on the axis of a 300 mm wooden dial turns once a day and lights one 30° sector at a time. Each sector carries a low relief that reads as plain texture in daylight, yet under the lamp casts a pixel cat in one of twelve poses, one for every two hours of a cat's day. The relief was not sculpted by eye but solved: from a target image of shadow, floor, half-lit and bright cells, every ray from the lamp was given a wall, a ramp or a tilted block, and the result was checked against a simulated lamp of finite size. A wall-mounted edition 35.5 mm deep completes the design.
