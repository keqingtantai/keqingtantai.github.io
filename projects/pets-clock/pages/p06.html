5. The Grid and Twelve Poses

Twenty wedges by fifteen rings per sector, four tones per cell: the whole visual language of the clock.

5.1Continuous grid

Rings spaced geometrically keep every cell the same shape.

Wedges are 1.5°; ring radii grow by 2.6 % per ring from 97.9 to 143.8 mm. Arc width and ring depth then grow together from 2.56 to 3.76 mm, so cells near the rim are larger but never stretched, and all lines run continuously across the sector.

5.2Four tones

Shadow, half-lit, floor and bright.

Black cells are shadow. White is the plain floor. Grey is a raised face tilted away from the lamp, about half as bright as the floor. Red is a face tilted toward it, two to three times brighter: the eyes.

5.3Drawing rules

Rules that keep each cat buildable.

Grey and red cells sit inside black along their ray, so a shadow exists for them to stand out from. Cats are kept within about 27° where the pose allows, so that the outermost wedges, which fall in the soft edge of the beam, stay empty.
